\textbf{Glossary of terms}
\begin{description}[itemsep=4pt]
  \item[Clause category] One of the 41 types of contractual provision annotated in CUAD (e.g., \emph{Governing Law}, \emph{Non-Compete}, \emph{Cap on Liability}).
  \item[Contract-level split] A train/test partition that assigns whole contracts to one side, so no clause from a training contract can appear in the test set.
  \item[Presence classification] Deciding, for each clause category, whether a contract contains at least one clause of that category.
  \item[Span extraction] Locating the exact character span of a clause inside the contract text, formulated as extractive question answering.
  \item[Max-pooling (over windows)] Scoring every sliding window of a document and taking the maximum window score as the document-level prediction.
  \item[Retrieval ceiling] The upper bound that a retrieval step imposes on a downstream model's recall: clauses in windows the retriever never selects can never be found.
  \item[Weaker party] The contract signer with less bargaining power, from whose perspective the risk taxonomy is scored.
\end{description}

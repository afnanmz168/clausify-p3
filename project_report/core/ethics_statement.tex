\section*{Ethics Statement}
This project uses the Contract Understanding Atticus Dataset (CUAD), a public research dataset of 510 business contracts taken from public filings and labelled by legal experts for The Atticus Project. We did not collect or use any private, confidential or personal documents, and no people took part as research subjects. All experiments ran on our own computers. The outside models we fine-tuned (DistilBERT, FLAN-T5) and all the software libraries are used under their open-source licences, and every outside source is cited.

The system is a research prototype meant to help with contract review. It is not legal advice, and the app says so. The ethical risks of relying on its output (false comfort from missed clauses, too much trust by non-lawyers, rules that differ between countries, whose side the risk is judged from, and privacy in real use) are discussed in Section~\ref{sec:ethics}.
